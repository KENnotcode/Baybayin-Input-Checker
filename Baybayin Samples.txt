Invalid Baybayin Sentence
1. ᜋᜑᜎ᜔ ᜒᜃᜆ
   - MARK_WITHOUT_BASE at index 5 (DFA)
   - The second word starts with a kudlit and has no base letter

2. ᜐᜎᜋᜆ᜔᜔ ᜉᜓ
   - DOUBLE_VIRAMA at index 5 (DFA)
   - "salamat" has two killing marks in a row

3. ᜀᜃᜓ ᜀᜌ᜔ ᜉᜒᜓᜎᜒᜉᜒᜈᜓ
   - DOUBLE_KUDLIT at index 10 (DFA)
   - "pi" has two kudlits on one consonant

4. ᜃᜓᜋᜁᜈ᜔ ᜀᜃᜓ᜔
   - KUDLIT_THEN_KILLER at index 10 (DFA)
   - "ku" is followed by a killing mark

5. ᜋᜄᜈ᜔ᜇᜅ᜔ ᜀᜓᜋᜄ
   - VOWEL_TAKES_MARK at index 9 (DFA)
   - "u" is written as vowel A plus a kudlit, which independent vowels cannot take

6. ᜊᜑᜌ᜔ ᜈᜋᜒᜈ᜔ᜓ ᜁᜆᜓ
   - KUDLIT_AFTER_KILLED at index 10 (DFA)
   - A kudlit follows the killed "n" in "namin"

7. ᜋᜑᜎ᜔ ᜃᜒᜆa
   - NOT_IN_ALPHABET at index 8 (DFA)
   - A Latin "a" is mixed into Baybayin

8. ᜋᜑᜎ᜔ ᜃᜒᜆ!
   - NOT_IN_ALPHABET at index 8 (DFA)
   - Punctuation is outside Σ by default (valid if the ASCII-punctuation flag is on)

9. ᜋᜑᜎ᜔ ᜃ᜔ᜆ᜔
   - NO_VOWEL_IN_WORD at index 5 to 8 (word rules)
   - The second word is only killed consonants ("k-t"), so it has no vowel sound

10. ᜊᜑᜌ᜔ ᜃᜒᜈ᜔ᜈ᜔ᜌᜒᜆ᜔
    - DOUBLED_KILLED_CONSONANT at index 7 to 10 (word rules)
    - "n-n" is a doubled killed consonant
